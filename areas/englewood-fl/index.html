Englewood straddles two counties — we cover both

Englewood sits across the Sarasota–Charlotte county line, so neighbors a few streets apart deal with different county offices. For drywall work, it doesn’t matter which side you’re on — we cover both.

Englewood and the Lemon Bay area took heavy storm surge and wind-driven rain in recent hurricane seasons. Common jobs here are flood cuts, water-stained ceilings and replacing drywall behind removed baseboards and cabinets.
