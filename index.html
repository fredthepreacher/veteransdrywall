Straight answers

Frequently asked.

Do you offer free drywall estimates?

Yes — estimates are free. Call (941) 527-5924 or use the estimate form with a few photos. Most estimates come back within one business day.

Is Veteran Drywall licensed?

Yes. Veteran Drywall LLC holds Florida license # SCC131152687, which you can verify on the Florida DBPR license lookup. We can provide documentation for HOAs and property managers on request.

Who owns Veteran Drywall?

Steven McPherson, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran with more than a decade of service and four overseas deployments. He runs the business with his crew, Mike and Rick.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in North Port and serve Port Charlotte, Venice, Sarasota, Englewood and Punta Gorda across Sarasota and Charlotte counties. Nearby but not listed? Call and ask.

Do you handle small drywall repairs?

Yes. No job is too small — doorknob holes, a single crack, nail pops and small ceiling stains are a core part of what we do.

Can you match my existing wall or ceiling texture?

Yes. Texture matching is our specialty — knockdown, orange peel, skip trowel, popcorn and smooth Level 5 — so repairs blend in instead of standing out.

Do you repair water-damaged drywall?

Yes. Once the source of the leak is fixed and the area is dry, we remove damaged drywall, rehang, finish, match texture and seal with stain-blocking primer.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings?

Yes — we scrape and refinish smooth or with a new texture. Homes built before the mid-1980s should have the texture tested for asbestos first.
