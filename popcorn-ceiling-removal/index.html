Popcorn ceiling removal

Popcorn ceilings, scraped & modernized.

Out with the bumpy acoustic texture. We scrape, repair, and refinish smooth, knockdown or orange peel — with the room protected start to finish.

Quick answerPopcorn ceiling removal means scraping off the acoustic texture, repairing the drywall underneath, and refinishing it smooth or with a new texture. In homes built before the mid-1980s, the texture should be tested for asbestos by an accredited lab before it is disturbed. Veteran Drywall LLC removes and refinishes popcorn ceilings across North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice, Sarasota and Punta Gorda.

How we do it

  1. Test if needed. For older homes, get the texture tested for asbestos before anything is scraped.
  2. Seal the room. Floors, walls and fixtures are covered in plastic; HVAC vents sealed.
  3. Scrape & repair. Texture removed, seams and dings repaired.
  4. Skim & finish. Smooth skim coat or new texture applied and sanded.
  5. Prime, paint & clean up. Primed (and painted if included), then plastic comes down and debris is removed.
Asbestos warning: popcorn ceilings installed before the mid-1980s may contain asbestos. Don’t scrape, sand or drill into it until a sample has been tested by an accredited lab. If it tests positive, removal must be handled by a licensed asbestos abatement contractor. See the EPA’s guidance on asbestos in the home.

Popcorn in Southwest Florida homes

Many older homes in Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda and Venice were built during decades when popcorn ceilings were standard, so we see a lot of them — especially in homes being updated for sale or rental. Newer North Port and Wellen Park-area homes rarely have it.

Popcorn Ceiling Removal FAQ

Does my popcorn ceiling have asbestos?

You can’t tell by looking. Homes built before the mid-1980s should have a sample tested by an accredited lab before removal. Newer homes are much less likely to contain it, but testing is inexpensive peace of mind.

How long does popcorn ceiling removal take?

A typical room takes a couple of days including drying time between coats; a whole house is scheduled room by room.

Can you remove painted popcorn?

Yes, but paint makes the texture harder to soften and scrape, so it takes longer. We’ll factor that into your estimate.

Smooth or textured after removal?

Your choice. Smooth looks the most modern but shows every imperfection under strong light; a light knockdown is more forgiving.
