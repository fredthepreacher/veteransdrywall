Quick answerAlong with drywall, Veteran Drywall LLC handles interior and exterior painting, baseboards, casing, crown molding, framing and finish carpentry across North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice, Sarasota, Englewood and Punta Gorda — so repairs and remodels are finished by one accountable crew.

What we fix

Interior paintingWalls and ceilings with sharp cut lines.
Exterior paintingStucco and trim, prepped for Florida sun.
Baseboards & casingInstalled, caulked and painted.
Crown moldingTight miters and clean reveals.
FramingWall changes, soffits and blocking.
Finish carpentryCustom details that finish a remodel.

How we do it

  1. Prep. Patching, caulking and sanding before any paint.
  2. Protect. Floors, furniture and fixtures covered.
  3. Prime & paint. The right primer, then finish coats.
  4. Trim out. Tight joints, filled nail holes, clean caulk lines.

Painting cost

National averages for interior painting run about $2 – $8+ per sq ft (HomeGuide). When painting follows our drywall work, it’s usually quoted together.

Painting, Carpentry & Trim FAQ

Do you only paint after drywall work?

Painting is usually the finishing step of our drywall and stucco jobs, but ask about standalone painting and trim projects too.

Do you install baseboards after flood cuts?

Yes — after flood cuts we can rehang drywall, finish it, and reinstall or replace baseboards and paint.
