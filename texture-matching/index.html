Our specialty

Texture matching so good it vanishes.

Patching drywall is one thing. Making the repair invisible is the craft. We reproduce the exact texture already on your wall or ceiling — knockdown, orange peel, skip trowel, popcorn or smooth.

Quick answerTexture matching means reproducing the existing wall or ceiling pattern over a drywall repair so it blends in. Veteran Drywall LLC matches knockdown, orange peel, skip trowel, popcorn and smooth (Level 5) finishes — plus exterior stucco — across North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice, Sarasota, Englewood and Punta Gorda.

What we fix

KnockdownThe most common Florida texture — splattered compound flattened with a knockdown knife into soft, flat-topped islands.
Orange peelA fine sprayed texture with a subtle dimpled look, common in newer construction.
Skip trowelHand-troweled, irregular ridges — popular in higher-end and Mediterranean-style homes.
Popcorn / acousticMatched for small patches, or removed entirely. See popcorn ceiling removal.
Smooth / Level 5A full skim coat for a flat, modern finish with no texture at all.
StuccoExterior patches matched to the surrounding profile and sand finish.

How we do it

  1. Identify the texture. We look at pattern size, spacing, height and how the paint sits on it — two “knockdowns” can look very different.
  2. Test before we commit. Pressure, nozzle, compound thickness and knockdown timing are dialed in on a test board first.
  3. Feather the edges. The new texture is blended out past the patch so there is no hard line between old and new.
  4. Prime & paint. Primer evens out absorption; paint that matches in sheen finishes the illusion.
Honest note: texture that’s been painted many times, or paint that has faded in sunlight, can make any repair visible until the whole wall is repainted. We’ll tell you up front if repainting the full wall (corner to corner) is the only way to make it truly disappear.

Common textures in Southwest Florida homes

Across North Port, Port Charlotte and Punta Gorda, knockdown on walls and ceilings is the most common finish we see, followed by orange peel in newer builds. Older Port Charlotte and Venice homes often still have popcorn ceilings. Coastal condos in Sarasota and Venice are more likely to have smooth or skip-trowel finishes.

Texture Matching FAQ

Can you match my existing texture exactly?

In most cases, yes. We test the pattern on a board first. If fading or many layers of paint mean a perfect blend isn’t possible, we’ll tell you before we start and recommend repainting the full wall.

What is the difference between knockdown and orange peel?

Orange peel is a fine sprayed dimple texture. Knockdown is heavier splatter that’s flattened with a knife into smooth-topped islands. Knockdown is the most common texture in Southwest Florida homes.

Can you make a textured wall smooth?

Yes. A Level 5 skim coat covers existing texture for a flat, modern finish.

Do you match ceiling texture too?

Yes — ceiling repairs are blended the same way, including popcorn patches when you’re keeping the popcorn.
